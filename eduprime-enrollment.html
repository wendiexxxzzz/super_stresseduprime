Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, courses, and the student experience.

When does enrollment open for the next semester? +
Enrollment for the 2026–2027 academic year is currently open. Early bird applications are prioritized for course selection.
How many courses can I enroll in per semester? +
Students may enroll in a maximum of 5 courses (18 credit hours) per semester. A minimum of 3 courses (9 credit hours) is required for full-time status.
Can I change or withdraw from a course after enrolling? +
Yes, course modifications are allowed within the first two weeks of the semester. After that, a formal withdrawal process is required through your student portal.
What documents do I need for enrollment? +
You'll need a valid ID, previous academic records, and a recent 2x2 photo. Additional requirements may apply depending on your program.
